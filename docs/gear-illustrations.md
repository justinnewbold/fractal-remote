# Gear illustrations

Some of the gear images in `public/gear/` are original hand-drawn style illustrations by Justin Newbold, created 2026-10-03. They were made for gear where no licensed photo could be found.

- Each illustration is modeled on the real unit (layout, colours, controls and logo placement) but is **not a photograph** of it.
- Every illustration is flagged `is_illustration=yes` in [`public/gear/sources.csv`](../public/gear/sources.csv), with `copyright_holder=Justin Newbold` and `licence=Original illustration`. Real photos are flagged `is_illustration=no`.
- When a licensed photo turns up, replace `public/gear/{slug}.jpg` and update that slug's row in `sources.csv` (including setting `is_illustration=no`), then remove the slug from the table below.
