What’s new

Fractal Remote

What each update changed, newest first. Updates reach the app on their own; Settings → About → Updates says which one you are running.

2 November 2026

Version 1.89.23

  • Settings → About opens straight onto Updates, What’s new, Help with this app and The small print, with no extra tap.

31 October 2026

Version 1.89.18

  • New: What’s new, under Settings → About, lists what each update changed.
  • On a wide screen, such as a tablet or a folding phone opened out, Previous and Next sit at the two ends of the buttons under Play.
  • Shorter Bluetooth pages, and the heat warning is shown only for the FM3, the one unit that needs it.

30 October 2026

Version 1.89.14

  • Less to read: the Bluetooth page, Settings, the purchase page and Edit say the same things in fewer words.

29 October 2026

Version 1.89.12

  • One screen for feedback and problem reports: type what happened, add a photo or video, and choose whether to include your email and the app’s log.
  • New, and only if you say yes: share which gear works with Fractal Remote, to help build a list of everything that works. It is not linked to you. Settings → Troubleshooting → Share what gear works.
  • Settings sections fold open and closed, so the list is shorter.
  • The quick preset jump numbers are no longer cut off on smaller screens.
  • Edit’s beta note has an X, and stays closed once you close it.

28 October 2026

Version 1.89.5

  • Saving works when the computer app runs on a Raspberry Pi that starts it at power-on.
  • Quick preset jumps start at 0.
  • After Bluetooth connects and the unit answers, the app goes straight back to Play.

26 October 2026

Version 1.89.3

  • The Bluetooth page starts with two steps: pick your unit, then Connect. How to connect, Troubleshooting and What Bluetooth can’t do each have a page of their own, with every way to hook up each unit and what to buy.
  • If Bluetooth won’t connect after you plug the adapter back in, close the app completely and open it again.

25 October 2026

Version 1.89.0

  • New: Bluetooth, from version 1.89.0. With the full version and a Bluetooth MIDI adapter on your unit, control an AM4, FM3, FM9, Axe-Fx III or VP4 with no computer (on a VP4, presets and scenes). The computer is still the best way: Bluetooth has more lag and fewer features. Settings → My rig → Bluetooth.
  • Report a Bluetooth problem, on the Bluetooth page, sends a report straight from the app.
  • On a tablet, the app turns sideways with the screen. (Android)
  • On the Apple Watch, an AM4’s or VP4’s scenes and pedals fill the screen. (iPhone and iPad)
  • Previous and Next, and the names under them, are drawn smaller rather than cut off.

24 October 2026

Version 1.88.6

  • Scene names are as big as their tiles have room for, one size across the whole grid.
  • Play’s top bar says which unit you are on, and on which computer. Tap it to switch.
  • A problem report can carry a screenshot.
  • On the Apple Watch, an AM4 amp’s pedal says Boost on, Boost off or Amp off. (iPhone and iPad)
