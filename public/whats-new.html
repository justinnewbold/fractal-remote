What’s new

Fractal Remote

What each update changed, newest first. Updates reach the app on their own; Settings → About → Updates says which one you are running.

10 November 2026

Version 1.90.17

  • On a tablet turned on its side, or a folding phone opened, Play can now share the screen: tap Edit, Setlists or the preset name and it opens beside Play, and the tuner takes a narrow strip instead of covering everything. Each side has Swap and Full screen, and Two sides brings the other back.

9 November 2026

Version 1.90.17

  • Getting connected is simpler: Connect a computer is now three numbered steps, and the Bluetooth and Raspberry Pi setup guides are short pages with links to what to buy.
  • On a tablet or other big screen, Play’s scenes and effects now fill the whole screen as big tiles, as close to square as they fit, instead of wide stretched bars.
  • In Edit, a block’s knobs now come straight under its channel buttons, with Copy channel, on and off, and the model below the knobs.
  • On the Bluetooth page, a USB MIDI cable that’s plugged in, like the U2MIDI Pro on an FM3, now has its own Use the USB cable button, even while the app is still trying a Bluetooth adapter. The FM3’s note now says that cable plugs straight into your phone or tablet.
  • On a tablet, the walkthrough and the Unlock page now fill the screen, with bigger words, pictures and buttons, and the Unlock page opens wider.
  • From version 1.90.0 of the app, Edit opens over Bluetooth on an AM4, FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III: turn knobs, change models, switch blocks on and off and pick channels, and a unit on a USB cable does the same. Changes play right away; save on the unit to keep them.

8 November 2026

Version 1.89.77

  • On an Axe-Fx III over Bluetooth or a USB cable, Play now shows the NAM blocks from firmware 33 (NAM and NAM 2) and the IR Player block, and turns them on and off like your other effects.
  • Play’s Edit button no longer goes missing on Bluetooth or a USB cable: tap it and it tells you why it needs a computer there. In the demo, the VP4’s Edit no longer has knobs, chain editing, Modifiers or renaming, like a real VP4. And through a computer, an AM4’s Edit no longer shows a Modifiers section it can’t use.
  • If a Bluetooth MIDI footswitch, like an M-VAVE Chocolate, gets picked as your adapter, the app no longer takes it on its own, and the Bluetooth page says it’s a footswitch and to pick the adapter on your unit instead. Through a computer, an FM3’s Footswitches page also shows what each switch picks.
  • In the phone app, the Unlock page now shows the free trial and Unlock before the list of what it unlocks, so there’s less to scroll. Where the free 7-day trial is offered, it’s now also on the walkthrough’s Connect my real rig card and on the demo’s card on Play.
  • Your first and last name are now optional when you make an account; add them any time under Settings → your account in the phone app, where the reset link now names the email it goes to. The walkthrough’s last button now says Let’s go, and the glass circles behind the top buttons sit centered on their icons.
  • On the website, How it works now opens on Bluetooth, as in the app: a Bluetooth adapter on your unit and the free app on your phone or tablet, with download buttons. Tap Computer for the computer way. Forgot password? now works: its email opens a page to choose a new password.

7 November 2026

Version 1.89.66

  • Bluetooth connects more reliably: if your adapter connects but your unit stays silent, the app reconnects once by itself (or tap Connect again), each unit remembers its own adapter, the app tells you when an adapter is plugged into a different unit, and no computer warnings show while you're on Bluetooth.
  • Getting started is quicker: after you sign up, start the trial or buy, one card asks how you connect, Bluetooth adapter first or a computer. Connect over Bluetooth shows while the app looks for your computer, with far less to read. The website's welcome now says Bluetooth is in the phone and tablet apps.
  • Reports now take up to five pictures plus one video. And in the phone app, Settings → your account → tap your email lets you add your name and a profile photo. The photo shows in a little circle beside Live at the top, and tapping the circle takes you straight there.
  • Start 7-day free trial on an iPhone or iPad whose Apple ID already bought the full version now says so: the full version moves to the account you're signed in to, and no trial starts. (iPhone and iPad)
  • Tablets: held upright, Play is the phone's own layout, just bigger; on its side, your scenes sit on the left with the pedals two across beside them, row for row. Smaller tablets now get this too.
  • Try the full version free for 7 days with your own rig: tap Start 7-day free trial on the purchase page. While it runs, Play shows how many days are left, and when it ends everything you set up is kept. The demo is still free and unlimited.

6 November 2026

Version 1.89.55

  • On a tablet or a foldable opened up, Play now uses the whole screen: your scenes on the left and your chain on the right, lined up row for row, with the preset across the top and Previous and Next at the two ends of the bottom row. Setlists is at the end of the preset bar.
  • Settings → About → Updates now shows Checking… while it looks, then the time it last checked, and when a new version is ready, its card has What’s new, which takes you straight to the list of what changed. The Help make Fractal Remote better? card is now three short lines, with a link to exactly what’s sent.
  • Which unit (tap your unit’s name on Play) is now one card per unit, showing the computer it’s on, whether it’s connected, and the preset and scene it’s on. And switching to a computer with no unit plugged in no longer makes the screen flicker back and forth.
  • Blocks newer than the app, like the Axe-Fx III’s new NAM block, are now left alone in Edit: they show as New block, their spot is never shown as free, and nothing can be put over them.
  • Plug your unit in by USB cable while connected another way and the app notices: from a Bluetooth adapter it switches by itself, through the computer it asks first. Unplug the cable and, after a few seconds, it goes back the way it was connected before, and back to the cable when it’s plugged in again.
  • After using a USB cable, the Bluetooth page’s Connect gets your Bluetooth adapter back instead of waiting for the cable, and Use a Bluetooth adapter instead sits beside Use the computer instead. Connect is bigger and blue, with the Bluetooth symbol, and Stop is only offered for what’s really connected.

5 November 2026

Version 1.89.46

  • Settings → Colors: tap a look to pick it, then Apply. You’re taken back to Play, with Undo there for a couple of seconds if you change your mind.
  • Creating an account now asks for your first and last name, so we know who we’re helping when you write to us. If you already have an account, nothing changes and you won’t be asked.
  • Share what gear works (Settings → Troubleshooting) now notes a unit on a USB cable as a USB cable, how long your unit took to connect and, through a computer, how quickly it answers a tap. If you said yes before, it asks you once more. It’s still off unless you say yes, and never linked to you.

4 November 2026

Version 1.89.41

  • The computer app now notices within a couple of seconds when your unit is switched off and on, or its USB cable is unplugged and plugged back in, and reconnects by itself, so you no longer have to restart the computer app.
  • In the phone app, buttons, bars and pop-ups have a new frosted glass look, with rounder buttons and a soft light along the top. Prefer the old look? Choose Classic under Settings → Colors → Style. Sunlight keeps the glass solid for bright stages, and in Light mode the small gray labels are easier to read.
  • On the website and the computer app, Tuner, Edit and Tap at the bottom of Play are now the phone app’s taller buttons, with the tempo under Tap.
  • On an iPhone or iPad, a unit you’ve plugged in by USB cable is now called USB cable instead of Bluetooth, and Connect uses it without opening Apple’s Bluetooth screen. (iPhone and iPad)
  • The Bluetooth page now has Use a different adapter, so you can switch adapters even while it’s still trying the one from last time. When that one isn’t answering, it names another adapter it can see, with a button to use it, and Forget the adapter under Troubleshooting works with Bluetooth on too.

3 November 2026

Version 1.89.32

  • On the website and the computer app, the top bar, Settings and Play now match the phone app’s: one button that says Live and your unit, Settings in sections that fold with Done on every page, and Play’s headings, Refresh names, scene names as your unit spells them, and Tuner, Edit and Tap at the bottom.
  • The walkthrough shows both ways to connect, Computer (the best way) or Bluetooth, its pages are numbered 1 to 4, and the box before Unlock says both ways. The purchase page lists every Bluetooth adapter, and Playing with no internet says Bluetooth needs none.
  • On the computer app, signing in under Settings → Account now turns the phone remote on too, so your phone finds the computer straight away after you switch accounts. On a Raspberry Pi set to start by itself, closing the app’s window no longer stops it saving presets you send from your phone.
  • If the part of the computer app that talks to your unit stops, it now starts again by itself on every computer, and the phone remote comes back on once it’s running. Links in the computer app open in your normal web browser.
  • The website and the computer app’s window carry their own fonts now, so they work with no internet. The support page names Feedback’s tick boxes the way the app shows them, and the page for getting the app leads with the store.
  • Show the walkthrough no longer leaves you stuck at the sign-in screen: when you’re already signed in, Back to your own rig at the top takes you straight back. And when you press Previous or Next quickly a few times and one press doesn’t go through, Play now shows the preset your unit is really on.

2 November 2026

Version 1.89.27

  • Settings → About opens straight onto its rows, the Bluetooth page’s three help pages open properly again, and Phone & computer has its Connect over Bluetooth button back and checks again for your computers and their units each time it opens.
  • Stars and setlists you make while trying the demo now stay in the demo, never changing your real ones or your account’s. The end of the demo walkthrough and the sign-in screen say you can connect with a computer or, with no computer, by Bluetooth, and that the computer is the best way.
  • Restore a purchase, tapped while signed out, says first that the purchase belongs to the account you bought it with, and offers to sign in to it. On a VP4, the purchase page and the demo say presets and scenes go as the messages a MIDI foot controller sends, and the FM3 warning on the Bluetooth page reads more plainly.
  • On an iPhone or iPad set to another language, the Bluetooth page no longer mistakes its own Wi-Fi MIDI connection for your adapter. With no adapter connected, it now says so. (iPhone and iPad)
  • Settings → Siri shows the newer way to stop Siri using the app on iOS 27.2 and later, and the Apple Watch tips say plainly to keep your iPhone unlocked on Play for every tap to land, and to tap again if the first one after a long pause doesn’t. (iPhone and iPad)
  • The back button inside Settings goes up one page instead of jumping straight to Play, and on the feedback screen it asks before throwing away what you wrote. (Android)

25 October 2026
