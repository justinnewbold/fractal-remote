What’s new

Fractal Remote

What each update changed, newest first. Updates reach the app on their own; Settings → About → Updates says which one you are running.

18 November 2026

Version 2.0.5

  • From version 2.0.0 of the app, over Bluetooth or a USB cable on an AM4, FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III: Edit chain moves, adds and removes blocks. Over a Bluetooth adapter the FM3, FM9 and Axe-Fx III are read again after each change, which takes up to a minute.
  • From version 2.0.0, over Bluetooth or a USB cable: Rename preset and scenes, Copy a scene onto another and Copy channel are in Edit, as they are through a computer.
  • From version 2.0.0, the tuner on an FM9 or Axe-Fx III over Bluetooth no longer needs Send Realtime Sysex switched on at the unit: when the unit sends no readings, the app asks it for the pitch.

17 November 2026

Version 2.0.1

  • From version 2.0.0 of the app, turn your phone on its side on Play to see your scenes on one side and your pedals on the other, as big square buttons. The smallest phones stay upright.
  • From version 2.0.0, over Bluetooth or a USB cable on an AM4, FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III: Save in Edit and on Play saves over the preset your unit is on, and on an empty preset Quick build puts in the blocks you tap, in order, and joins them up. Keep your own Starter chains in Settings → My rig → Starter chains.
  • From version 2.0.0, on an FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III over Bluetooth or a USB cable: your blocks show in the unit’s own order, Edit’s knobs follow a knob turned on the unit, and the volume button and each scene’s level are there. And Edit shows a block’s list settings, such as Bypass Mode, under its knobs.
  • From version 2.0.0, on an Axe-Fx III: a Capture button on the NAM block in Edit picks which capture it plays, and holding a NAM block on Play offers TONE3000, to browse captures, preview one through your own rig and get it onto your unit. Through a computer, Play names the NAM, IR Player and other newer blocks.
  • From version 2.0.0, Settings → Metronome has a flash round the edge of the screen with or without sound, a choice of where the click sounds, and Line up the light and click. On every version, its light now sits a little in from the edge, so its corners are no longer cut off where the screen is rounded.
  • From version 2.0.0, on Apple Watch: choose which pages it shows and their order in Settings → Apple Watch, a new Tempo page with Tap, and hold a pedal for its channels and knobs, turned with the Digital Crown. And Apple’s Bluetooth screen has its Done button back. (iPhone and iPad)
  • In the chain editor on an FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III, dragging blocks along a row now joins the row up again and checks it. Where it can’t, it says why, and a row it finds cut offers Join the row wherever the app can join it.
  • The computer app on a Raspberry Pi now puts in its own updates. It waits until nobody has used the Pi for half an hour (no phone connected, nobody touching its screen, nothing changed on the unit) and until a new version has been out for a day. From version 2.0.0, Phone & computer has Update now.

16 November 2026

Version 1.91.2

  • Knobs now point the way your unit’s do: lowest at the bottom left, highest at the bottom right, and a middle setting such as Pan lights up from the top. From version 1.90.0 of the app, Edit on an Axe-Fx III over Bluetooth or USB also has knobs for the IR Player, Vocoder, Crossover, Tone Match and Dynamic Distortion.
  • Over Bluetooth, when your adapter says it’s connected but nothing comes back from your unit, Play now says so, and Fix it step by step opens the Bluetooth page, which walks you through the resets that cure it one at a time: restart the adapter, turn Bluetooth off and on, forget it and pair it again.
  • In Edit on an FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III, a knob that reads back differently isn’t sent again another way (that could throw Bit Reduce to its lowest), an unanswered change says it couldn’t be checked, and the Axe-Fx III’s NAM knobs on channels B, C and D work. Over Bluetooth or USB, whole-number settings read right.
  • On a Raspberry Pi, the computer app now puts in a waiting update by itself when the Pi is switched on, even with its window open. Before, it waited for the app to be quit, and switching the Pi off at the wall isn’t a quit, so it could sit on an old version.
  • In the app on a phone or tablet, dragging the volume slider no longer says there is no output level to move yet. The level you let go on is now checked against your unit, and Save knows the preset changed, as they already were for the − and + buttons.
  • Over Bluetooth or a USB cable on an FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III, a preset with no blocks in it no longer says the unit did not answer in time once you have switched a block on or off. And in the metronome, tapping the same choice again after your unit didn’t take a change now really asks the unit again.

15 November 2026

Version 1.90.82

  • In the chain editor, the blocks you can add are now a menu instead of a wall of buttons. Tap Block to add and the list comes up over the page, in order by name, with the find box staying at the top while you scroll. In Edit on a narrow screen, the title is no longer squeezed when Confirm changes shows.
  • Bluetooth setup and troubleshooting are now by unit and by adapter. Pick your unit, then tap your adapter’s tab, and you see only the parts, steps and fixes for that pair instead of one long list for all of them. The Yamaha MD-BT01 has its own tab on an FM3, FM9 and Axe-Fx III.
  • On an FM3, the app no longer says never to use other Bluetooth adapters. It warns that an adapter may overheat on an FM3 and is used at your own risk, and it now lets you connect whichever adapter you have. The WIDI Uhost with a CME U2MIDI Pro is still the one that stays cool.
  • In the chain editor, Add now answers under the block you tapped: where the new block went, or that its row is full and what to do instead. On an AM4, each block is shown in its real slot, and adding or taking one out changes the right slot. It was one slot along.
  • Bluetooth: when your adapter is connected but your unit isn’t answering, the note under the pill on Play now says so, with what to check, instead of saying Connected. For a WIDI Master it names its own two plugs: the larger one in MIDI OUT and the small one in MIDI IN.
  • Over Bluetooth or a USB cable on an Axe-Fx III, the NAM, IR Player, Vocoder, Tone Match and Dynamic Distortion blocks now have their own colors on Play instead of gray. On an FM9, the Vocoder and Tone Match do too. On an FM9 or Axe-Fx III, the browser’s Starter chain now builds on the third row of an empty preset.

14 November 2026

Version 1.90.72

  • Over Bluetooth or a USB cable on an Axe-Fx III, the NAM block has its own picture on Play: a small neural network in rainbow colors.
  • On an iPhone or iPad on iOS 26 or later, Apple’s Bluetooth screen has no button that says Done, so the app now says how to close it: swipe that screen down. (iPhone and iPad)
  • After you send feedback or a problem report, the button at the top now says Done instead of Back.
  • Bluetooth: when your adapter connects but your unit isn’t answering, the Bluetooth page now offers Use a different adapter right there, in case your unit is on another adapter than last time. And after Use a different adapter, the app no longer goes straight back to the adapter you had.

13 November 2026

Version 1.90.53

  • On an iPhone or iPad, when your Bluetooth adapter has dropped off, Look for the adapter again now shows straight away instead of after fifteen seconds. (iPhone and iPad)
  • Over Bluetooth or a USB cable on an FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III, a preset with no blocks in it no longer says the unit did not answer in time.
  • On an Axe-Fx III over Bluetooth or a USB cable, Edit now has the NAM block’s knobs: Input Gain, Bass, Mid, Treble, Bright, Bright Cap and Level. The capture is still chosen on the unit.
  • In Edit, tapping the number under a knob now highlights it, so you can type a new number straight over it.
  • When Bluetooth won’t connect, the app now says the first thing to try: unplug the Bluetooth adapter for a few seconds and plug it back in.
  • Over Bluetooth or a USB cable on an Axe-Fx III, Play now shows the Crossover, Vocoder, Tone Match and Dynamic Distortion blocks, and turns them on and off like your other effects. On an FM9, the Crossover, Vocoder and Tone Match too.

12 November 2026

Version 1.90.41

  • Amp & pedal names is now its own button at the top of Settings, right under your profile, instead of inside Help.
  • Bluetooth → What Bluetooth can’t do now lists everything that still needs the computer: saving, moving blocks, modifiers, renaming, copying a channel, and choosing a cab on an FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III.
  • On Play, the line beside Chain now says Hold for quick edit: hold an effect and its knobs come up over the A to D channels.
  • On a tablet or other big screen, the button that puts a second screen beside Play is now called Split View (it was Two sides).

10 November 2026

Version 1.90.31

  • New in Settings, under My rig: Connect your unit. Pick a Bluetooth adapter, USB cable, computer or Raspberry Pi and get short steps for that one (the Raspberry Pi’s are in the app now). Troubleshooting asks the same, so you see only the fixes for your way in, and they’re shorter.
  • Edit now opens with a block’s knobs already showing, more of them at once. Edit chain, Modifiers, Rename and Amp & pedal names moved into the three-dot button beside Done, and the magnifier finds a control. A block’s Level, the Output’s too, can be typed under the knobs, never below -20 dB.
  • Hold an effect on Play and its knobs now come up above the A to D channels, so you can make a quick change without opening Edit. On a tablet or other big screen on its side they open beside Play instead. One tap still turns the effect on or off.
  • New: Stage view shows your preset, scene and what’s next, big enough to read from a stand, with the tuner as a strip. Open it from Settings → Play screen. And on a tablet or other big screen, Play’s scenes and effects now fill the whole screen as big tiles, as close to square as they fit.
  • Setlists: in All presets, hold a preset to add it to a setlist (its color is on the same sheet). And each setlist song can carry notes, such as lyrics, chords or cues, shown beside Play and in Stage view. Notes stay on the device you wrote them on.
  • On a tablet turned on its side, or a folding phone opened, Play can now share the screen: tap Edit, Setlists or the preset name and it opens beside Play. Its buttons are in the top bar. Drag the handle on the line between the sides to resize, or tap it to swap. A very wide screen can add a third side.

9 November 2026

Version 1.90.15

  • Getting connected is simpler: Connect a computer is now three numbered steps, and the Bluetooth and Raspberry Pi setup guides are short pages with links to what to buy.
  • On a tablet or other big screen, Play’s scenes and effects are now square tiles that spread across the screen, instead of wide stretched bars.
  • In Edit, a block’s knobs now come straight under its channel buttons, with Copy channel, on and off, and the model below the knobs.
  • On the Bluetooth page, a USB MIDI cable that’s plugged in, like the U2MIDI Pro on an FM3, now has its own Use the USB cable button, even while the app is still trying a Bluetooth adapter. The FM3’s note now says that cable plugs straight into your phone or tablet.
  • On a tablet, the walkthrough and the Unlock page now fill the screen, with bigger words, pictures and buttons, and the Unlock page opens wider.
  • From version 1.90.0 of the app, Edit opens over Bluetooth on an AM4, FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III: turn knobs, change models, switch blocks on and off and pick channels, and a unit on a USB cable does the same. Changes play right away; save on the unit to keep them.

8 November 2026

Version 1.89.77

  • On an Axe-Fx III over Bluetooth or a USB cable, Play now shows the NAM blocks from firmware 33 (NAM and NAM 2) and the IR Player block, and turns them on and off like your other effects.
  • Play’s Edit button no longer goes missing on Bluetooth or a USB cable: tap it and it tells you why it needs a computer there. In the demo, the VP4’s Edit no longer has knobs, chain editing, Modifiers or renaming, like a real VP4. And through a computer, an AM4’s Edit no longer shows a Modifiers section it can’t use.
  • If a Bluetooth MIDI footswitch, like an M-VAVE Chocolate, gets picked as your adapter, the app no longer takes it on its own, and the Bluetooth page says it’s a footswitch and to pick the adapter on your unit instead. Through a computer, an FM3’s Footswitches page also shows what each switch picks.
  • In the phone app, the Unlock page now shows the free trial and Unlock before the list of what it unlocks, so there’s less to scroll. Where the free 7-day trial is offered, it’s now also on the walkthrough’s Connect my real rig card and on the demo’s card on Play.
  • Your first and last name are now optional when you make an account; add them any time under Settings → your account in the phone app, where the reset link now names the email it goes to. The walkthrough’s last button now says Let’s go, and the glass circles behind the top buttons sit centered on their icons.
  • On the website, How it works now opens on Bluetooth, as in the app: a Bluetooth adapter on your unit and the free app on your phone or tablet, with download buttons. Tap Computer for the computer way. Forgot password? now works: its email opens a page to choose a new password.

7 November 2026

Version 1.89.66

  • Bluetooth connects more reliably: if your adapter connects but your unit stays silent, the app reconnects once by itself (or tap Connect again), each unit remembers its own adapter, the app tells you when an adapter is plugged into a different unit, and no computer warnings show while you're on Bluetooth.
  • Getting started is quicker: after you sign up, start the trial or buy, one card asks how you connect, Bluetooth adapter first or a computer. Connect over Bluetooth shows while the app looks for your computer, with far less to read. The website's welcome now says Bluetooth is in the phone and tablet apps.
  • Reports now take up to five pictures plus one video. And in the phone app, Settings → your account → tap your email lets you add your name and a profile photo. The photo shows in a little circle beside Live at the top, and tapping the circle takes you straight there.
  • Start 7-day free trial on an iPhone or iPad whose Apple ID already bought the full version now says so: the full version moves to the account you're signed in to, and no trial starts. (iPhone and iPad)
  • Tablets: held upright, Play is the phone's own layout, just bigger; on its side, your scenes sit on the left with the pedals two across beside them, row for row. Smaller tablets now get this too.
  • Try the full version free for 7 days with your own rig: tap Start 7-day free trial on the purchase page. While it runs, Play shows how many days are left, and when it ends everything you set up is kept. The demo is still free and unlimited.

6 November 2026

Version 1.89.55

  • On a tablet or a foldable opened up, Play now uses the whole screen: your scenes on the left and your chain on the right, lined up row for row, with the preset across the top and Previous and Next at the two ends of the bottom row. Setlists is at the end of the preset bar.
  • Settings → About → Updates now shows Checking… while it looks, then the time it last checked, and when a new version is ready, its card has What’s new, which takes you straight to the list of what changed. The Help make Fractal Remote better? card is now three short lines, with a link to exactly what’s sent.
  • Which unit (tap your unit’s name on Play) is now one card per unit, showing the computer it’s on, whether it’s connected, and the preset and scene it’s on. And switching to a computer with no unit plugged in no longer makes the screen flicker back and forth.
  • Blocks newer than the app, like the Axe-Fx III’s new NAM block, are now left alone in Edit: they show as New block, their spot is never shown as free, and nothing can be put over them.
  • Plug your unit in by USB cable while connected another way and the app notices: from a Bluetooth adapter it switches by itself, through the computer it asks first. Unplug the cable and, after a few seconds, it goes back the way it was connected before, and back to the cable when it’s plugged in again.
  • After using a USB cable, the Bluetooth page’s Connect gets your Bluetooth adapter back instead of waiting for the cable, and Use a Bluetooth adapter instead sits beside Use the computer instead. Connect is bigger and blue, with the Bluetooth symbol, and Stop is only offered for what’s really connected.
