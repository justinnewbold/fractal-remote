It won’t install over the Google Play copy. Android says App not installed, because the two are signed differently. And buying the full version needs the Google Play copy.

Tap the button on the phone itself. Android asks once whether this browser may install apps — say yes. It installs over an earlier copy from this page without removing anything, and your setlists and pairing stay where they are.

Built for 64-bit Android, which is every phone made this decade. If an older handset with no Google Play copy on it says App not installed, that is why — tell me and I will add the older one back.
