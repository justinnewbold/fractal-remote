Set up a Raspberry Pi for Fractal Remote

Fractal Remote · last updated 9 November 2026

A Raspberry Pi is a small computer, about the size of a deck of cards. Set up once, it sits by your Fractal unit and runs the Fractal Remote app by itself, so your phone controls the unit with no laptop at the gig. You need the full version on your phone.

On this page: what to buy · setting it up · an AM4 on a Pi 3 or 4 · if something goes wrong · for gigs · retiring an old Pi

What to buy

Easiest: one kit. The CanaKit Raspberry Pi 5 Starter Kit, 4 GB, with the official case has the Raspberry Pi 5, the official case with its fan, a power supply and a memory card.

Or the parts:

  • A Raspberry Pi 5, 4 GB.
  • The official Raspberry Pi 27W USB-C Power Supply. A phone charger isn’t enough for a Pi 5.
  • A case with a fan, such as the official Raspberry Pi 5 case.
  • A microSD card of 32 GB or more. It gets wiped, so a blank one is fine.

And the USB cable for your unit: USB-A to USB-B (a “printer cable”) for an FM3, FM9 or Axe-Fx III, or USB-A to USB-C for an AM4. It has to be a data cable, not a charge-only one.

To set it up you also need a Mac, Windows PC or Linux computer with a microSD card reader, and your wifi’s name and password.

The links go to Amazon in the US. Fractal Remote gets nothing from them.

Already have a Pi?

  • Good: a Pi 5 or Pi 4 with 2 GB or more, or a Pi 400, 500 or 500+.
  • Works, but slow: a Pi 3 Model B or B+, or any Pi with 1 GB. Use its official power supply. With an AM4, it needs one setting.
  • Not recommended: a Pi Zero 2 W or Pi 3 Model A+ (too little memory).
  • Won’t work: a Pi 2, any Pi 1, and the first Zero, Zero W and Zero WH (32-bit only).

Setting it up

1. Put Raspberry Pi OS on the card, from your computer

  1. Download Raspberry Pi Imager from raspberrypi.com/software and open it. (A Mac needs macOS 13 or newer.)
  2. Put the card in the reader. Choose your Device (your Pi), the OS Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit) (the first one, marked Recommended), and your card under Storage.
  3. Fill in the next screens: a Hostname such as pedalboard; under Localisation, your country’s capital, then your time zone; a User name and a password of letters and numbers only (write them down); and your Wi-Fi name and password exactly. On a Pi 3, use a 2.4 GHz network.
  4. On Raspberry Pi Connect, switch on Enable Raspberry Pi Connect, click Open Raspberry Pi Connect, sign in with a free Raspberry Pi ID, and click Create auth key and launch Raspberry Pi Imager. Leave SSH off.
  5. Click Write, then I understand, erase and write, and wait for Write complete!. If a Mac says the disk isn’t readable, click Ignore, never Initialize.

2. Start the Pi, and open its desktop

  1. Put the Pi in its case (plug the fan’s cable into the socket marked FAN) and the card in the slot underneath. Plug in the power within six hours of writing the card. The first start takes several minutes.
  2. On your computer, go to connect.raspberrypi.com, sign in, open Devices, then Connect via → Screen sharing. The Pi’s desktop opens in your browser.

3. Put Fractal Remote on the Pi

  1. On the Pi’s desktop, open its web browser (Raspberry Pi menu → Internet), type fractal.newbold.cloud/downloads into its address bar, and download Raspberry Pi and other ARM — AppImage. Close the Pi’s browser.
  2. Open the File Manager (Raspberry Pi menu → Accessories) and its Downloads folder. Right-click the file ending in arm64.AppImage, choose Properties → Permissions, set Execute to Anyone, then double-click it and choose Execute.
  3. Choose I already have an account and sign in with the same account as your phone. Then open Settings (⚙) → About and tick Start when this computer turns on.
  4. Plug your unit into the Pi with its USB cable and switch it on. Open Fractal Remote on your phone: it finds the Pi and your unit.

4. Test it

Raspberry Pi menu → Shutdown → Shutdown. Unplug the Pi, plug it back in, and wait a few minutes: your phone finds your unit again, with no screen at all. That’s it.

No other computer? Set it up with a screen on the Pi

Plug a TV or monitor (micro-HDMI to HDMI on a Pi 5 or 4), a USB keyboard and a mouse into the Pi. Use a card that came loaded with Raspberry Pi OS (64-bit), or on a Pi 4 or 5 hold Shift as it starts to install it over a network cable. Answer the Pi’s own questions: your country, a user and a password of letters and numbers only, and your wifi. Then do part 3 above on the Pi’s screen, unplug the keyboard, mouse and screen, and do part 4.

An AM4 on a Pi 3 or Pi 4

On a Pi 3 or Pi 4 (the Pi 400 too), give the Pi one setting so your AM4’s scene names come through. Without it the scene buttons show numbers. A Pi 5 doesn’t need it.

Give the Pi the setting
  1. On the Pi’s desktop, open Terminal (Raspberry Pi menu → Accessories → Terminal).
  2. Type echo options snd-seq-midi input_buffer_size=65536 | sudo tee /etc/modprobe.d/fractal-remote-midi.conf exactly, press Enter, and type the Pi’s password if it asks.
  3. Restart the Pi: Raspberry Pi menu → Shutdown → Reboot.

To check it, type cat /sys/module/snd_seq_midi/parameters/input_buffer_size: 65536 means it’s on; 4096 means restart once more. To undo it, type sudo rm /etc/modprobe.d/fractal-remote-midi.conf and restart.

If something goes wrong

  • The Pi doesn’t show up in Connect: give it ten minutes. If it still isn’t there, the wifi name or password was probably typed wrong, or the card was written more than six hours earlier: write the card again.
  • The Pi says your password is wrong: it’s the Pi’s own password, not your computer’s or your wifi’s. Write the card again with a password of letters and numbers only.
  • Double-clicking the app does nothing: check the file ends in arm64.AppImage and Execute is set to Anyone.
  • The phone says there is no computer: sign in to the same account on both, check Start when this computer turns on is ticked, and give the Pi a few minutes after switching on.
  • The app can’t find your unit: use a data cable, not a charge-only one, and switch the unit off and on.

More is on the support page.

For gigs

  • Internet: your phone’s hotspot is surest. Add it once at home from the Pi’s desktop, and the Pi joins it by itself. A venue’s wifi with a sign-in page won’t work.
  • Power: plug its own power supply into the wall, not a pedal power supply or a power bank.
  • Shutting down: on a Pi 5, press its power button twice, then unplug it. On other Pis, Raspberry Pi menu → Shutdown, then unplug it.

Retiring an old Pi

On the old Pi, open Fractal Remote, then Settings → My rig → Phone & computer → Phone remote → Turn off, and under About untick Start when this computer turns on. Giving the card away? Erase it first with Raspberry Pi Imager. A new Pi gets a different Hostname.
