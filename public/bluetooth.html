Bluetooth

Fractal Remote · last updated 31 October 2026

Bluetooth is in the app, from version 1.89.0. With a Bluetooth MIDI adapter plugged into your unit’s MIDI jacks, the app controls it with no computer. It is part of the full version, under Settings → My rig → Bluetooth. Get the app from the App Store or Google Play.

There are two ways to connect. The computer is the best one. Bluetooth needs no computer, but it has more lag and fewer features.

What it does

Presets, scenes, effects on and off, channels, tempo and the tuner (on a VP4, presets and scenes). No editing or saving over Bluetooth: for those, use the desktop app.

Which units

It is offered on the AM4, FM3, FM9, Axe-Fx III and VP4. So far it has been tried on the AM4, the FM3 and the Axe-Fx III. It has not been tried yet on an FM9 or VP4.

On an FM3, use the CME U2MIDI Pro, never the C2MIDI Pro or a WIDI Master: both got very hot on one.

Setting it up

The Bluetooth setup guide says what to buy for your unit, how it plugs in, and what to try when it doesn’t connect. The parts are sold separately, by other companies.

Something not working? Report a Bluetooth problem, on the app’s Bluetooth page, sends us a report in one tap. Or use the support page, or email justinnewbold@gmail.com.
