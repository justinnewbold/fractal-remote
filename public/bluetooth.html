Bluetooth (beta)

Fractal Remote · last updated 11 October 2026

Bluetooth (beta) isn’t available in the app. It is still being tested, and may be offered in a later version of the app.

Your phone connects to your Fractal unit through the desktop app: the unit plugs into a Mac, Windows or Linux computer with a USB cable, and the computer runs the Fractal Remote desktop app. Get the desktop app.

There is nothing to buy for it now. If you turned Bluetooth (beta) on while it was in the app, the app goes back to the computer by itself once it has updated. If you have just installed or updated the app and still see a Bluetooth (beta) card, it goes away by itself once the app has updated.
